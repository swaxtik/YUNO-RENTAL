Plans and billing

Clear rental information before you message

Daily plan

Charged by 24-hour blocks from pickup date and time. Pickup today 10 AM and return tomorrow 10 AM is 1 day.

Weekly plan

Weekly rates are applied automatically in the estimate when your duration reaches 7 days.

Documents

Carry a valid driving license and one ID proof. Deposit, if needed, is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Before pickup

Vehicle availability, final amount, exact pickup point, and helmet request are confirmed by chat.

Helmet request

Mention one or two helmets while booking so the handover is prepared before you arrive.

Fuel usage

Fuel is on actuals. Refuel as needed and clarify the return fuel level before starting.

Return timing

Return on the confirmed time to avoid extra day charges and keep the next booking smooth.

Longer rentals

Weekly or custom plans can be discussed directly through WhatsApp based on availability.

FAQ

Common questions

How is 1 day calculated?

One rental day is one 24-hour block. If pickup is 10 AM today and return is 10 AM tomorrow, the estimate shows 1 day.

Is fuel included?

Fuel is on actuals. You can refuel as needed during your ride.

Can I extend the rental?

Yes, subject to availability. Message YUNO RIDE as early as possible before your return time.

What do I bring?

Bring a valid driving license and one ID proof. Deposit or additional checks are confirmed before pickup.

Do I pay online?

Payment is normally handled at pickup after availability and final amount are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can I book for someone else?

You can enquire for someone else, but the rider must show their own valid driving license during pickup.

What if I return late?

Late return can create extra charges because rental billing uses time blocks. Message early if you need an extension.

Where is the pickup point?

The exact Kundapura pickup point and directions are shared after your booking is confirmed.
