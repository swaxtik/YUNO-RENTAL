Rental guide

Know before you go.

Clear answers help every YUNO rider start confidently.

What documents do I need?

A valid original driving licence and a government-issued photo ID. Additional verification or security terms may apply.

Is fuel included in the price?

No. Fuel is on actual usage. Note the handover fuel level and return the scooter at the agreed level.

How are rental days counted?

We count elapsed rental dates with a one-day minimum. For example, 11 August to 12 August is one day.

What happens if I return late?

Late returns may attract additional charges. Message YUNO as early as possible if your plan changes.

Who pays traffic fines or damage costs?

The rider is responsible for traffic violations, negligent damage and other charges under the confirmed rental terms.

Can I travel outside Kundapura?

Tell us your route before booking. Outstation use requires specific approval and may have separate conditions.
